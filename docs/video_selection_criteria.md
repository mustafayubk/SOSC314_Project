# Video Selection Criteria

## Goal
Build a comparable sample of YouTube videos across genres so that differences in comments are more plausibly attributable to video type rather than arbitrary selection.

## Genres
- TED-style talks (educational/explanatory talks)
- Short films (standalone narrative fictional films)
- Personal opinion videos (first-person commentary/argument)

## Inclusion criteria (applied to all genres)
1. Publicly accessible YouTube video with comments enabled
2. English-language content (title/description primarily English; comments largely English)
3. Minimum comment threshold: target ≥ 2,000 total comments (ensures sufficient discussion)
4. Standalone content (not trailers, compilations, reposts, or short clips)
5. Not primarily music videos (to avoid lyric-related comment patterns)

## Time stratification
Videos are selected across three time bins to avoid concentration in one period:
- 2000–2010
- 2010–2020
- Post-2020

## Sample size plan (staged scaling)
- Pilot: 1 video (≈30–50 comments) to validate fields and pipeline
- Baseline corpus: 9 videos (3 per genre × up to 500 comments each)
- Final corpus (end of project): 45 videos (15 per genre × up to 500 comments each)

## Notes
If a chosen video fails collection (e.g., comments disabled), it will be replaced with the next eligible video using the same criteria.
